Week in 6 points

  1. Palm oil rose for a second week despite record Malaysian stocks: exports and India support prices.
  2. Malaysian exports for 1–15 October are up 43–58% on September.
  3. India imported the most palm oil in seven months in September; sunflower oil imports fell to a four-year low.
  4. Brent hit $110: Hormuz attacks and Russian refinery strikes support biodiesel demand.
  5. Black Sea: ships sunk and hit off Bulgaria and Romania; war-risk insurance is rising.
  6. The US EPA proposed full reallocation of exempted biofuel volumes; soyoil still fell on the slow timeline.

What it means for palm oil 3

Prices

Exports and crude are offsetting record stocks. El Niño and Indonesian losses are upside risks; a crude sell-off is the main downside risk.

analyst view
Markets

In India, palm keeps a strong discount to sunflower oil ahead of Diwali. In the EU, traceability is becoming a condition of access.

analyst view
Routes

Black Sea disruption limits sunflower oil supply. This creates room for palm in India, Türkiye and Central Asia.
