Анализ региональных рынков

Haze and El Niño Add Supply Risk

Fires in Indonesia have burned at least 200,000 hectares across six provinces, and on October 9 Malaysia closed schools in Kuala Lumpur, Putrajaya and several states because of haze (Al Jazeera). Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim asked for an urgent meeting of ASEAN ministers. The reports reviewed do not quantify any effect on palm oil output.

Palm Oil Hits a 12-Week Low, Then Recovers Part of the Loss

Malaysian palm oil futures fell for six straight sessions to October 2, when the benchmark contract closed at 4,533 ringgit/t, the lowest close since July 10, on weak export data and expectations of higher stocks (Reuters via Business Recorder). The contract slipped again on October 7 to 4,524 ringgit on concerns about record inventories (UkrAgroConsult), then rebounded to end the week of October 9 at 4,592 ringgit, up 1.26%.

FAO Index Confirms Palm-Led Strength in September

The FAO Vegetable Oil Price Index averaged 198.6 points in September, up 0.9% from August and 18.3% above a year earlier. Palm oil prices rose for a fourth consecutive month on strong import demand and concerns over production prospects in Southeast Asia, while sunflower oil prices declined for a third consecutive month on expectations of ample Black Sea supplies (FAO, October 2).

Record Malaysian Palm Oil Stocks

MPOB's October 12 release put end-September inventories at 3,510,897 t, above the Reuters poll median of 3,373,737 t. Output of 2,116,950 t was up 16.48% from August and exports of 1,120,395 t were down 12.13% (Finimize, citing MPOB). A Bloomberg survey reported by The Star had expected stocks of 3.45 million t; an analyst quoted there said stocks should stay near record levels through the first half of 2027, while El Niño and Indonesia's biofuel mandate could tighten the global market later.

US Soybeans: WASDE and Trade Headlines

CBOT November soybeans were near 1,292 cents/bu on October 9. USDA's October 9 WASDE raised 2026/27 US soybean ending stocks by 5 million bushels to 315 million, left the season-average price at $12.00/bu and put global ending stocks at 124.28 million t (DTN). In US–China trade, China's tariff-cut list covers soybean oil and meal but not commercial soybeans, which still face an additional 10% tariff, and no effective date has been given (Reuters).

Ukraine's Minimum Export Price Floor Lowered for the Fourth Quarter

In September Ukraine's Economy Ministry set the minimum export price for sunflower oil at $1,350/t CPT, about $225/t above market offers of around $1,125/t. On September 30 the Cabinet (Resolution No. 1192) extended through December the 0.714 correction factor first applied in August, which lowers the binding floor for grains, oilseeds and sunflower, soybean and rapeseed oils. The resulting October floor for sunflower oil was not found in the sources reviewed.

Merchant Ships Struck Off Romania and Bulgaria

On October 5 a drone strike set the Royad Mammadov, a Turkish-owned carrier loaded with corn, on fire in Romania's exclusive economic zone, and the vessel sank. On October 6 the Togo-flagged Alfa Watan and the Palau-flagged Able were hit more than 100 km off Bulgaria; Alfa Watan sank with crew reported missing, and all 18 crew of Able were rescued. It was the first reported drone attack on merchant shipping in Bulgaria's exclusive economic zone. Ukraine's president attributed the attack to Russia, while Romanian and Bulgarian authorities described the incidents as drone strikes without naming the origin (Reuters; Kyiv Independent; UkrAgroConsult).

EU and Bulgarian Response

Bulgaria's prime minister called the strike a gross violation of international law (Reuters). European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen said the Black Sea situation was increasingly concerning, and on October 7 the EU called on Russia to stop attacks on commercial vessels and announced cooperation with Bulgaria and Romania, including satellite imagery of the affected sea areas (Sofia Globe; USM).

Export Flows and Domestic Prices

Ukraine's government said average daily agricultural export shipments in the week before September 30 were about 48 thousand t, against about 144 thousand t in the first half of September, while domestic purchase prices fell (milling wheat down 16%, feed wheat 17%, barley 14%, corn 8%, rapeseed 5%). Sunflower seed purchase prices fell to UAH 16,500–17,500/t by October 7 as the harvest advanced (UkrAgroConsult; GrainTrade).

Sunflower Oil Export Outlook for 2026/27

Market estimates presented at the Globoil conference in Mumbai put Ukraine's sunflower oil exports in October–March 2026/27 at roughly 300 thousand t per month, about 50% below normal flows, because of damage to port and storage infrastructure and elevated maritime risk. The same reporting says Russia's 2026/27 exports could fall by up to 38% (CMB News, October 2).

USDA: Russia Overtakes Ukraine

USDA's September Oilseeds: World Markets and Trade report estimated 2025/26 sunflower oil exports at 4.2 million t for Russia and 4.036 million t for Ukraine, ending Ukraine's 20 seasons as the largest exporter. Ukraine's share of world exports fell from about 35% to 30%. For 2026/27 USDA forecasts Ukraine at about 5 million t and Russia at 5.1 million t (kurs.com.ua, citing USDA).

Russia Export Duty Capped Through December

Global Trade Alert records that Russia capped export duties on sunflower oil at RUB 7,748/t and on sunflower meal at RUB 312/t, applicable until December 31, 2026 (Ministry of Agriculture decision of September 25).

Sunflower Oil Exports Nearly Halted in September

Russia shipped only about 5 thousand t of sunflower oil in September against more than 200 thousand t a year earlier. From January to August, Russian vegetable oil exports were 4.69 million t (up 4%), of which sunflower oil was 3.19 million t (up 2%), with India (913 thousand t) and Turkey (718 thousand t) the main buyers (CMB News, October 5; trade press, not an official statistic).

Kazakhstan

Record Oilseed Crop Pressures Seed Prices

Kazakhstan is expected to harvest more than 5 million t of oilseeds this season. Domestic sunflower seed prices have fallen to KZT 162,000/t including VAT and could drop to KZT 150,000/t, according to the Grain and Oilseed Crops Kazakhstan research bureau. Russian seed is offered to Kazakh crushers at about KZT 120,000/t, while the cost of local seed is approaching KZT 160,000/t (UkrAgroConsult, October 9).

Record Sunflower Oil Exports in 2025/26

In marketing year 2025/26 Kazakhstan's sunflower oil production rose 18.2% to 874.9 thousand t, exports rose 34% to an all-time high of 815.4 thousand t, and imports fell 15.9% to 125.7 thousand t (UkrAgroConsult, October 7).

Seed Export Duty Stays

The 20% export duty on sunflower seed (minimum €100/t) continues to apply outside the EAEU, and authorities do not currently plan to revise it, arguing that it secures raw material for domestic crushers and supports oil exports. Seed exports outside the EAEU fell 6.6% to 210 thousand t in 2025/26, with China taking 78.9% (UkrAgroConsult, October 7 and 9).

Uzbekistan

Customs Fee Cut and VAT Offset From October 1

From October 1, 2026 fees for customs clearance, phytosanitary certificates, fumigation and certificates of origin fall by 30%, and low-risk importers holding valid VAT registration certificates may offset VAT payable on imported goods. From January 1, 2027 customs value controls for low-risk importers will take place after release of the goods (UzDaily, September 2 and October 2026). The earlier edition of this report described the 30% reduction as applying to export customs duties; the sources describe fees.

Logistics and Supply Security

Middle Corridor Status

An Astana Times analysis of October 12 says the container hub at Aktau, developed with China's Lianyungang Port, is complete, dredging at Kuryk is complete and similar work at Aktau is planned this year. The Digital Trade Corridor platform is integrated with the railway systems of Kazakhstan, Azerbaijan and Georgia, but the Caspian Sea remains a critical link because cargo must be transferred between rail, sea and road.

Black Sea Shipping Risk

The October 5–6 attacks on merchant ships extend the shipping risk into the exclusive economic zones of NATO members Romania and Bulgaria. Bulgarian authorities said the incidents complicate navigation and could further raise insurance and transport costs (UkrAgroConsult). Two Russian grain terminals at Novorossiysk had been put out of action by Ukrainian drone attacks in August (Al Jazeera, citing Reuters).

Rerouting of Russian Exports

Trade press reports that alternative corridors through Baltic, Caspian and Far Eastern ports are only partly compensating for the near-halt in Black Sea sunflower oil shipments and carry higher costs (CMB News, October 5).

Regulatory Outlook

Application Date Unchanged at December 30, 2026

The EUDR applies from December 30, 2026 to large and medium-sized operators and to micro and small operators in the timber sector, and from June 30, 2027 to other micro and small operators. The Commission's May 4, 2026 simplification package confirmed that the application date would not be postponed again and that the regulation would not be reopened. No change to these dates was found in the sources reviewed for October 1–12 (law-firm summaries of the Commission package).

US Renewable Fuel Standard (RFS): EPA Reallocation Proposal Expected by End-October

On August 31 EPA decided 34 small-refinery exemption petitions for 2025, granting 18 full and 11 partial exemptions (about 1.76 billion RINs) and denying three, with two found ineligible. EPA said it would propose reallocating 100% of the difference between projected and actual exempted volumes into the 2026 and 2027 volume obligations before the end of October 2026, and extended the 2025 compliance deadline by 30 days to October 1, 2026 (S&P Global; Feedstuffs). No proposal had been found in the sources reviewed as of October 12.

EU MOAH Regulation: Formal Adoption Expected in October

EU member states backed the Commission's draft on maximum levels of mineral oil aromatic hydrocarbons (MOAH) in food at the SC PAFF on May 13, 2026. Formal adoption is expected in October 2026, subject to scrutiny by the European Parliament and the Council, with limits applying from January 1, 2027. Reported limits include 2.0 mg/kg for maize, rapeseed, sunflower, soybean and linseed oils and 4.0 mg/kg from 2027, falling to 2.0 mg/kg from 2028, for other oils and fats. How palm oil is classified within these categories should be checked against the final text. No confirmation of formal adoption was found as of October 12 (OFI; ChemLinked).

Indonesia Raises October Palm Oil Export Charges

Indonesia set the October 2026 crude palm oil reference price at $1,042.15/t, up 3.44% from September's $1,007.51/t. The export duty is $178/t and the export levy is 12.5% of the reference price, or $130.27/t, for exports from October 1 to 31. Branded RBD palm olein in packs of 25 kg or less carries an export duty of $48/t (Indonesian Trade Ministry, via RRI and IDNFinancials).

Malaysia: No Export Duty Waiver in the Budget

The Malaysian Budget presented on October 9 did not include the crude palm oil export duty waiver that the market had speculated about, which would have made Malaysian exports more competitive with those of Indonesia. Palm oil futures fell more than 1% on the day (Reuters via Business Recorder).
