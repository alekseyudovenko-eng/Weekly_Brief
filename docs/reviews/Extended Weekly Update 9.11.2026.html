Week in 6 points

  1. Palm oil fell 9.04% in a week to 4,258 MYR/t, the steepest weekly drop in nearly two years.
  2. Brent fell about 13% like-for-like to $98.32 on US–Iran deal hopes after the US midterms.
  3. India's October palm oil imports hit a 14-month high of 866,000 t; soyoil imports set a record.
  4. Russia claimed strikes on three cargo ships at Odesa and Chornomorsk; the claim is unverified.
  5. Russia–Ukraine–US talks are set for Abu Dhabi on 10–11 November, with no maritime item announced.
  6. Ukraine's sunflower harvest reached 10.34 mn t from 88.7% of the area.
