Week in 6 points

  1. Palm oil posted its first weekly loss in four weeks: record stocks and no export-tax decision weighed on prices.
  2. Malaysian exports stayed strong: surveyors put 1–25 October shipments up 47–74% month on month.
  3. Indonesia cut its November CPO reference price by 6% and its export duty to $124/t.
  4. Ukraine's sunflower harvest reached 9.8 mn t, and sunflower oil prices fell by about $100/t.
  5. A container ship sank after a drone attack off Bulgaria; Türkiye and Ukraine agreed to work on a grain corridor.
  6. Kazakhstan's ban on Russian sunflower seed is redirecting Russian trade to oil and meal.
