Week in 6 points

  1. Palm oil rose for a third week: BMD December +1.2% to 4,733 MYR/t despite record Malaysian stocks.
  2. Malaysian exports for 1–20 October jumped 58–78% month on month, and India is buying cheap palm oil before Diwali.
  3. Malaysia is considering a waiver of its 10% palm oil export tax; no decision yet.
  4. Brent hit a six-month high of $116.72 on Red Sea and Iran risks, supporting biodiesel demand.
  5. Black Sea sunflower oil exports in Sep–Dec are expected at about 2 mn t, the lowest in 11 years.
  6. Kazakhstan moved to halt Russian sunflower seed imports; Czech rapeseed crop is the smallest since 2005.
